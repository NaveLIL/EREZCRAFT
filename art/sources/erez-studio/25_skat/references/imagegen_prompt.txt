Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: approved-direction visual concept for an editable Minecraft EREZCRAFT vehicle model, SKAT / SK01.
Primary request: Design a beautiful, spacious expedition cargo catamaran for an extremely toxic burgundy ocean in a permanently dark fantasy dimension. Minecraft vanilla-scale block-built aesthetic, believable Create machinery, simple low-resolution textures, no photorealistic materials.
Subject: a 16-block-long, 9-block-wide twin-hull industrial exploration vessel. Two separated pointed stepped graphite armored pontoons with a clearly visible gap of toxic sea between them. Broad spanning cargo deck, large enclosed low cargo hold amidships, copper vertical ribs and muted teal accent panels. Low forward angular enclosed pilot cabin, broad dark cyan windows and warm headlights. Two large exposed air propellers high at the stern, both enclosed in strong polygonal safety rings; visible blades, stout mounts and copper drive machinery. Walkable side decks and guardrails. One compact folded cargo crane on the aft working deck. Large flush cargo hatches and a few neatly secured cargo crates suggest substantial storage. Safe practical proportions, visually disciplined detail, hull more prominent than top-heavy superstructure. Nothing flying, vessel rests in the sea.
Composition: one large three-quarter bow-side hero view occupying about 75 percent of a landscape canvas, complete stern propellers and both bows visible, generous margins. A small clean top-down cutaway inset on the right shows two longitudinal rows of cargo storage racks with an open central aisle and the forward cabin. The cutaway is a schematic design view of the SAME boat. Tiny unbranded Minecraft-scale person beside railing for scale. No labels or typography.
Lighting/mood: cinematic but legible, soft cool key light, warm copper highlights, dim teal instrument lights, subtle red water bounce. Calm poisonous red sea, distant dark rock silhouettes, almost no distracting background.
Constraints: clearly stepped cuboid geometry, restrained bevels, pixelated 16x16-style surfaces, strong identifiable twin hull silhouette, large useful cargo volume, enclosed cabin. No sails, no balloon, no rocket thrusters, no wheels, no military guns, no sci-fi smooth plastic, no 4K photographic textures. Concept only, no claims of working physics.
