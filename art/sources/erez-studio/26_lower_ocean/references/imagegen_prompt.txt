Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: practical visual target for a new lower toxic ocean material in the Minecraft modpack EREZCRAFT / Interstice. This is a material concept, not a ship illustration.
Primary request: Show a vast continuous unified sea of heavy dark burgundy toxic liquid. The main requirement is that it reads as ONE uninterrupted body of liquid, with long coherent surface currents running across many Minecraft blocks. Absolutely no visible tiled squares, repeated rosettes, crack network, glowing grid, checkerboard, hexagonal cells, rock plates, or lava pattern.
Scene: permanently dark otherworldly dimension. Elevated viewpoint from a small dark blocky stone shoreline at the lower left, looking across the sea. Horizon is near the upper fifth of the frame, distant angular rock islands are quiet silhouettes. The sea itself occupies at least 85% of the landscape image. No boats, people, buildings, monsters, text, diagrams, or UI.
Material: very deep wine-black and oxblood liquid. Broad low-contrast elongated swirls and interwoven flowing ribbons span long distances; they smoothly merge into one another, not separate puddles or outlined cells. Fine small ripples ride across these larger currents. Sparse thin patches of oily surface film catch dim muted copper and plum-violet reflections; the majority remains dark. Very rare subdued dusty-rose streaks gently fade back into the liquid, never bright red neon. A few tiny gas bubbles near the foreground are secondary. It feels dense and poisonous, yet visibly liquid, not tar, solid rock, cracked earth, molten lava or a glowing magical floor.
Style/medium: convincing in-game Minecraft material study, blocky 1-block-scale shoreline and low-resolution pixel-texture character on the sea, carefully restrained specular highlights, legible realistic fluid behavior within vanilla-style game art. Do not use 4K photographic microtexture or cinematic ocean foam. No enormous physical waves, no breakers, no white sea foam. The shape of the ocean stays mostly calm; the life comes from color flow, film, fine ripples and reflections.
Lighting and palette: dim neutral cool ambient light reveals the burgundy without making everything black; restrained soft reflections trace the moving fluid. Keep copper and violet film localized and rare. No red sky bathing the entire scene in neon, no orange fire, no strong bloom.
Composition: one single panoramic landscape image, all edges full bleed, uninterrupted broad view of the liquid. Make the continuous large-scale motion the clear visual subject, rather than spectacular scenery.
