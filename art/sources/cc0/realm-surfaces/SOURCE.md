# Coastal and alpine mineral textures

Original unused `sand_ugly_2.png`, `snow.png`, and `gravel.png` from
[16×16 Block Texture Set](https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-block-texture-set)
by ARoachIFoundOnMyPillow, CC0 1.0. File/archive hashes are in `sources.json`;
the legal text is included. Preparation changes the colour palette only,
preserving pixel coordinates, source colour classes, resolution and alpha.

The living torch and harvested-bud inventory geometry use the existing original
TideSprout flower model, unchanged textures and UVs; no new bud bitmap is made.
