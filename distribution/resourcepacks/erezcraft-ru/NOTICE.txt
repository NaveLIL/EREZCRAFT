EREZCRAFT Russian localization supplement

This standalone resource pack is licensed under GNU AGPL version 3 only.
The surrounding Interstice project retains its existing license.

Includes adapted translations from:
- RTF Extension 2.7.0: Guardian_50, MomentariyModder and the
  Russian Translations and Fixes contributors. AGPL-3.0-only.
- Rechiseled Russian Translation v3: leha7cool. MIT.
- Applied Guide Translations 1.21.1: zilibobiksus. AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Mekanism Russian translations: Mekanism contributors. MIT.
- Modded Minecraft Localization Resource Pack: RushanM and contributors. MIT.
- Existing translations from the audited mod versions, reused within their namespaces.

Upstream URLs, immutable versions and hashes are recorded in sources.json.
The editable ru_ru JSON and localized Markdown/Patchouli pages are the
corresponding source of this resource pack. Semantic corrections and their
English originals are additionally recorded in
distribution/localization/ru/reviewed-strings.json in the EREZCRAFT repository.

Original mod JARs and English resources are not included or modified.
